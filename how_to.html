How to use the app

Grow a better plant story.

Everyday Lilly works best when each photo is simple, steady, and repeatable. Use this guide to build a clean daily journal and turn it into a smooth timelapse.

Quick Start

  1. Choose one plant and one camera position.
  2. Take one photo at about the same time each day.
  3. Use alignment to match yesterday's pose.
  4. Add notes when something changes.
  5. Create a timelapse when the story is ready.
1

Set up the shot

Pick a stable place for the plant and try to keep the pot, background, and lighting consistent. A small mark on the table can help you put the pot back in the same spot.

2

Capture daily

Open the calendar, take today's photo, and save it. If you miss a day, continue the next day; the journal still works beautifully with small gaps.

3

Use alignment

When possible, compare the new camera view with the previous photo. Matching the stem, pot edge, or flower center makes the final timelapse feel calmer and more magical.

4

Add notes

Use notes for watering, sunlight changes, new leaves, repotting, or anything you want to remember. Notes make the gallery more useful than photos alone.

5

Create and back up

Generate a timelapse or export a backup when you want to save the full calendar. Keep backups somewhere safe before switching phones or deleting the app.

Best lighting

Soft daylight is friendlier than harsh direct sun. Try a window spot that looks similar every day.

Best framing

Leave a little room above the plant so new growth does not get clipped as it becomes taller.

Best rhythm

A daily reminder helps, but the story is yours. A steady habit matters more than perfection.
