Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

At Everyday Dandelion, your privacy is our core principle. This app is designed to be mindful, simple, and completely private.

No Data Collection

Everyday Dandelion collects no personal data. We do not collect, store, share, or track any information about you, your location, or your usage of the app. We have no servers, no accounts, and no databases for your personal information.

Local Storage Only

All photos, videos, and personal moments you capture remain entirely on your device. We do not have access to your media. Your memories are processed locally and stored within the app's secure sandbox on your iPhone or Android device.

Offline by Design

The app operates fully offline. There are no third-party analytics, advertisements, or background data connections reporting back to us. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it through other apps.

Third-Party Services

If you choose to back up your device (e.g., using iCloud or Google Drive) or share your videos to social media, those processes are handled by your operating system and the respective services. Please refer to their privacy policies for how they handle your data.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out at contactus@everydaylilly.com.
