How to Play

1

Pick your class & enter your name

Teachers set up a Class Profile. Students spin the retro 3-letter wheel to enter their arcade name — like KID or ACE.

2

Listen to the question

The friendly host reads the question aloud in a soft, kid-tuned voice. Even non-readers can play fully independently.

3

Tap your answer

Four big, colorful buttons. Tap one — correct answers turn green and trigger confetti 🎊. Wrong answers reveal the right one so kids always learn.

4

Celebrate on the leaderboard!

After 10 questions, see your stars and score. Top scorers take the 🥇🥈🥉 podium on the class leaderboard — a huge motivator for the whole class.
