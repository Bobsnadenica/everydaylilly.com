Privacy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Local-first design

Who Wants to Be Smart? stores gameplay data locally on the device (for example: class names, player names, and scores).

No account required

Children do not need to create an account to play. The game is intended to work without personal sign-up.

Data usage

We use gameplay data only to power features such as quick play persistence and leaderboards on the same device.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact: contactus@everydaylilly.com
