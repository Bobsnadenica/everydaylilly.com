Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kid's Clock Game handles user data. We believe in complete privacy, simplicity, and transparency.

Information Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information whatsoever. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

Local Storage

Any settings or progress within the app are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never shared with anyone.

Third‑Party Services

No third‑party services, analytics, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs are used. The app is fully self-contained.

Children’s Privacy

Since the app collects no personal data at all, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.

Your Rights

Because no data is ever collected or stored outside your device, there is nothing for us to modify, access, or delete. You remain in full control of everything on your device.

Changes to This Policy

If the app ever adds features that require data handling, this policy will be updated accordingly. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
